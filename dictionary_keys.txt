The Haridasa Kosha
The Haridasa Kosha is a digital repository dedicated to the preservation and study of Dasa Sahitya—the devotional literature of the Haridasaru of Karnataka.
The Haridasa Kosha Project
The Haridasa Parampara
The Haridasa Sahitya represents a unique blend of high philosophy and accessible poetry. Originating with Sri Narahari Tirtha and Sri Sripadaraja, the movement reached its zenith during the era of the Vijayanagara Empire.
The Kosha is organized to facilitate both devotional practice and academic research, providing multiple lenses to view the Haridasa tradition.
